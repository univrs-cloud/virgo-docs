Once setup is finished, you manage the node by signing in at the name you gave it during setup, for example `https://spica.virgo.univrs.cloud`.

## Topics

1. [Authentication](/management/authentication/): signing in and out, and what is gated by an account.
2. [Users](/management/users/): the people who can sign in and their roles.
3. [Your profile](/management/profile/): your own name, email address and password.
4. [Network](/management/network/): the node's name, its network interface and trusted proxies.
5. [Settings](/management/settings/): notifications, location and the weather, fleet and power.
6. [Updates](/management/updates/): keeping virgoOS up to date.
